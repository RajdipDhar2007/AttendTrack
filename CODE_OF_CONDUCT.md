# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and welcoming; harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported to **rajdipdharcode@gmail.com**. Maintainers may remove
comments, issues or contributors that violate this code.
