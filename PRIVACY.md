# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

AttendTrack is an offline-first attendance tracker. This policy explains what data the app
handles. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device
Your profile (name, institution, department, semester), subjects, teachers, timetable, attendance
records, exams, study goals and settings are stored **only on your device** in a local database.
The developer cannot see this data.

## Data that leaves your device (only if you use these optional features)
| Feature | What is sent | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Assistant** | Your question plus a summary of your attendance, timetable and faculty | Google Gemini API (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`), using your own API key. Subject to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). |
| **Cloud backup / restore** | Your exported backup data | The configured backup endpoint, only when you tap backup |
| **JSON / PDF export** | Nothing is uploaded | Files are saved on your device and shared only where you choose |

If you never use the AI Assistant or cloud backup, the app does not transmit your data.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state**: AI Assistant and cloud backup.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, boot completed**: class reminders and end-of-day prompts, restored after reboot.
- **Vibrate**: haptic feedback.

## Your control
You can export, delete or reset your data from Settings, or remove it all by clearing app data or
uninstalling the app. Your Gemini API key is stored on-device and can be removed in Settings.

## Children
AttendTrack is intended for students and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Changes
Changes to this policy will be published in this file and noted in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
